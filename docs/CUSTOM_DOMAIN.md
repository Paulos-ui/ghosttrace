# Optional `.tech` domain: no need to pay immediately

The official Week 4 challenge lists **Best Use of a .tech Domain**, with a **$100 prize** for that category. Requirements: claim a `.tech` domain **through Hacktoberfest**, make the project live on it, and link the actual domain in your DEV submission.

Official promotion: https://hacktoberfest.com/my/promos

**Cost disclosed by the organizer:** first year FREE through the promotion, **$29.99 per year at renewal**. Tax, eligibility, promotional availability, renewal settings and any premium-name surcharge must be checked on the claim checkout before confirming. Domain availability is unverified.

Suggested names to check, *not guaranteed available*: `ghosttrace.tech`, `ghosttracelab.tech`, `theghosttrace.tech`, `ghosttracearchive.tech`.

1. Sign in to Hacktoberfest and open the promotions page.
2. Claim the `.tech` promotion if eligible.
3. At checkout, verify the price **before** confirming and consider disabling automatic renewal if you don't want the year-two charge.
4. In Vercel, open your GHOSTTRACE project > Settings > Domains and add the domain you actually obtained.
5. Vercel will display the required DNS records (A/ALIAS/CNAME/TXT depending on apex/subdomain and ownership). Enter the **exact values Vercel shows** in the domain registrar's DNS page. Don't guess the record values.
6. Verify DNS and SSL in Vercel; test from an incognito browser.
7. Use the `.tech` URL in your DEV article and list **Best Use of a .tech Domain** under Prize Categories only after it actually works.

This is optional for the overall contest. Do not let domain setup block the project submission.
